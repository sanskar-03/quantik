FAQ

Clear answers before the transition.

Does Quantik replace existing payment infrastructure?

Its public positioning is as an additional quantum-safe security layer designed to work with existing payment infrastructure rather than requiring a complete replacement.

What is the quantum threat being addressed?

One focus is the “Harvest Now, Decrypt Later” risk: encrypted data collected today may be retained and potentially decrypted in the future as cryptographic capabilities evolve.

What cryptography is referenced publicly?

YesPanchi publicly references post-quantum cryptography and specifically ML-DSA / FIPS 204 in its Quantum Layer launch messaging.

Is Quantik a certified security product?

This website intentionally makes no certification claim. Standards references are presented as technical context, not as certification evidence.
